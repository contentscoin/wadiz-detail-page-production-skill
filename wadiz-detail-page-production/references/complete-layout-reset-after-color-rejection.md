# Complete Layout Reset After Color/Tone Rejection

Use when the user says the issue is **not color, palette, or tone**, but the **entire detail-page design structure**.

## Trigger signals

- “색상이 문제가 아니라 전체디자인을 완전 새롭게”
- “색만 바꾼 것 같다”
- “구조 자체를 다시 해”
- “전체 레이아웃이 마음에 안 든다”
- Rejection after a palette/metaphor shift such as green/ivory → black/gold/private-club

## Core rule

Do **not** answer with another palette, theme, or surface metaphor. Treat it as a structural design failure.

The correction is not:

- black instead of green
- more gold accents
- different serif/sans font
- another card skin
- membership pass/barcode/ticket decoration

The correction is:

- new section logic
- new cut-by-cut visual language
- new reading rhythm
- new information representation
- new mobile scroll experience

## Reset workflow

1. Explicitly acknowledge that a color/tone reset was the wrong level of change.
2. Keep verified business facts, legal terms, price, CTA, and required claims stable.
3. Discard the repeated layout vocabulary:
   - repeated cards/boxes
   - left text + right photo
   - same logo/header position on every cut
   - numbered list panels
   - pass/barcode/certificate decoration reused across many cuts
4. Rebuild the page as a sequence of **different section languages**, for example:
   - ad poster hero
   - conversation/message bubbles
   - operation timeline
   - brand touchpoint map
   - check-in/boarding flow
   - calendar/scheduling section
   - magazine collage
   - invitation sheet
   - protocol/editorial split
   - casting/roster section
   - folder/tab checklist
   - purchase-condition screen
   - large-number condition list
   - post-round content pipeline
   - consultation application/CTA screen
5. Generate the full contact sheet and long preview before judging; one hero cut cannot prove a structural reset.
6. QA against these questions:
   - Does every row of the contact sheet show different layout mechanics?
   - Would a viewer still recognize the old design if colors were removed?
   - Are information cuts still persuasive, not just novel?
   - Are small support lines readable at 1080px mobile size?
   - Are price/terms/CTA unchanged and legally safe?
7. If the new structure is accepted, run an upload-refine pass:
   - enlarge support copy 5–10%
   - strengthen weak/empty information cuts
   - fix overlaps after adding content
   - regenerate ZIP after QA reports are written

## Upload-refine lessons

After a structural reset, the next pass should not invent yet another design direction. Instead, harden the chosen structure for upload:

- Improve weak flow cuts with short explanatory cards, but avoid returning to dense PPT boxes.
- Turn empty legal/terms sections into clear checklists or purchase-condition screens.
- Keep strong visual cuts unchanged unless they fail readability.
- Re-check contact sheet, the changed cuts, price cut, and final CTA.
- Rebuild the ZIP after adding QA reports and selection notes.

## Pitfalls

- A “private club” or “membership pass” concept can still be only a color/theme change if the section mechanics remain cards and panels.
- Do not defend earlier iterations. The user correction is a signal to change the abstraction level.
- Do not over-minimize: premium detail pages still need concrete selling information.
- Do not let generated visuals hide exact Korean/legal text. Rebuild final copy deterministically and QA line breaks, collisions, and clipping.
