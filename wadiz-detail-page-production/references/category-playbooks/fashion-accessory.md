# Fashion / accessory

Plan around silhouette, fit, material, styling situations, actual sizes/colors, construction details, care and exchanges. `fashion-accessory` is a compatibility alias for `fashion`.

Use wearing contexts, material/detail closeups, option/fit guidance and care/offer blocks according to the actual garment/accessory. Avoid a compulsory two-scene count or fixed 12 cuts when the product needs different coverage. Visual emphasis does not remove information needed to select size or care for the item.

Size measurements, material composition, color names and care instructions need seller/product sources and exact editable text. Avoid unsupported luxury/department-store comparison claims. Generated wearing scenes must be labeled in artifact truth metadata and compared against the actual fit/design references; they are not a real customer testimonial or fit test.

GIFs can switch actual color options, reveal construction details or illustrate styling steps. Generated frames may not fabricate stretch, fit or material durability proof. Keep essential sizing information available statically beside motion.

OpenCrab query: actual clothing/accessory type plus topic, wearing rhythm, size/option information, material proof, exchanges and useful motion. Reject furniture/food-only examples and untraceable fragments.
