# Food

Plan around eating occasion, actual contents, taste/texture supported by product material, portion, ingredients/allergens, storage/shelf life, and delivery. Distinguish ordinary food from any separately regulated claim category using the actual product documentation. Do not infer classification from a marketing name.

Possible sections are use occasion, product/contents reveal, ingredients, source-backed differentiation, serving sequence, options, storage, cautions, offer and delivery. Split or merge them for the actual product; 15 cuts is not mandatory.

Required facts where applicable: ingredient list and allergens, net quantity, serving instructions, storage conditions, dates/shelf life and temperature/delivery conditions. Missing facts remain unknown; omit unsupported health/functional claims. Never invent disease prevention/treatment, guaranteed diet outcomes, review results, certification marks or institution logos. Certification/test sections need actual documents/assets.

Use exact editable text for ingredients, allergens, serving quantities, dates and cautions. Food photography should preserve the actual product and serving amount. GIFs can reveal contents or serving steps; generated frames cannot prove health effects, freshness, nutritional results or real texture tests.

OpenCrab query: source-backed Wadiz food pages for the actual food type and topic, ingredient/storage/serving sections, safety questions, offer/delivery details, and relevant GIF placement. Reject furniture, beauty-only, metadata and untraceable fragments.
