# Beauty

Plan around intended use, customer situation, product identity, ingredient information, source-backed benefits, actual tests, how to use, skin/use cautions and offer. A beauty device also needs the tech compatibility/power/service requirements. Do not infer efficacy from another product or from an ingredient alone.

Possible sections are target situation, product reveal, formulation/mechanism explanation, actual evidence, application steps, packaging/option details, cautions and offer. Include certification/testing only when actual source documents exist. Adapt section count to available evidence and purchase concerns.

Required facts where applicable: actual ingredients, usage instructions/amount, relevant restrictions/cautions, product labeling and verified test context. Functional/regulated wording needs current applicable source review. Never invent therapeutic outcomes, guaranteed improvement, no-side-effects statements, reviews, tests, certifications or institution marks.

Use exact editable text for ingredients, usage and cautions. GIFs may illustrate dispensing/application order or options; use actual footage for claims about application texture or observed performance. Generated before/after cannot serve as efficacy proof. Product packaging/labels must match approved references.

OpenCrab query: source-backed Wadiz pages for the actual beauty product and topic, ingredient/test placement, usage instructions, anxiety/FAQ patterns and motion purpose. Reject food/furniture-only results, title-only rows and missing-source fragments.
